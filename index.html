Core modules

Everything you need for underwriting

Four integrated modules streamline commercial auto underwriting for everyone involved: brokers save time on follow-ups and rework, insureds save time by knowing exactly what to submit, and both sides share a clear checklist of outstanding files—so renewals move forward with fewer gaps and less back-and-forth.

  • IFTA Summarize

    Aggregates four quarters of IFTA returns into a concise workbook with charts, metrics, and narrative-ready summaries—formatted for fast underwriting review and renewal documentation.

  • Driver List Validation Premium

    Reconciles driver rosters and MVR abstracts to confirm completeness, alerts brokers to outstanding documents, and flags mismatches—such as abstracts received for drivers not on the list, or roster entries without a matching abstract. Surfaces higher-than-average risk profiles so nothing important is missed at renewal.

  • Vehicle List

    Ingests and normalizes client vehicle inventories, reduces extraneous fields, and standardizes records so underwriting captures only pertinent data.

  • Compliance Risk Identification Premium

    Collects and analyzes CVOR II and SMS reports, highlights trends, repeat violators, and score movements, and summarizes findings for ongoing portfolio monitoring and renewal risk reviews.

Process

How it works

Three steps—from onboarding the insured to renewal-ready insight—for brokers and the clients they serve.

  1. Step 01

    Set up an insured

    Create the insured in Underwritly and send a secure upload link. Documents arrive in one channel—cleaner than threads of attachments and lost email.

  2. Step 02

    Automated analysis

    Underwritly pulls renewal-critical information into a concise report: the essentials in one read, with exceptions and risk drivers easier to spot before you decide.

  3. Step 03

    Maintain

    Keep files and risk signals current through the policy term—so renewals move smoothly and material changes don’t surface only at bind.
